#+TITLE: MR-01 FX Mean-Reversion Research Specification
#+STARTUP: overview indent

* Status

Proposed canonical specification for issue #278.

This document defines Trader's first FX mean-reversion research
experiment before implementation or examination of the resulting
MR-01 event-study output.

The purpose is to test whether a statistical mean-reversion phenomenon
exists. It does not define a trading strategy.

* Research Question

Do unusually large normalized deviations of an FX pair's H1 closing
price from its recent mean predict subsequent movement back toward
that mean?

The primary hypothesis is:

- large negative normalized deviations should be followed, on average,
  by positive forward returns;
- large positive normalized deviations should be followed, on average,
  by negative forward returns;
- the tendency toward the mean should become stronger as the magnitude
  of the deviation increases.

A failure to observe this pattern is a valid research result.

* Observation Definition

For each closed H1 bar at time t:

  M[t] = rolling arithmetic mean of Close over the previous N bars

  S[t] = rolling standard deviation of Close over the same N bars

  Z[t] = (Close[t] - M[t]) / S[t]

The current bar is included only according to the exact rolling-window
semantics established by MR-02. MR-02 must make those semantics
deterministic and explicit.

No observation is emitted until the rolling window is ready.

If rolling standard deviation is zero, the observation is invalid and
is excluded rather than producing NaN or infinity.

* Initial Parameters

These values are fixed for the first experiment and are not optimized.

** Bar interval

H1.

** Rolling window

N = 20 H1 bars.

This is a starting research assumption, not a claim that 20 is optimal.

No alternate lookback is tested in MR-01 through MR-04.

** Z-score buckets

Each valid observation belongs to exactly one bucket:

- Extreme negative: Z < -2.0
- Moderate negative: -2.0 <= Z < -1.0
- Neutral:          -1.0 <= Z <= 1.0
- Moderate positive: 1.0 < Z <= 2.0
- Extreme positive: Z > 2.0

The bucket boundaries are fixed before results are examined.

** Forward-return horizons

Measure forward close-to-close return at:

- 4 hours
- 12 hours
- 24 hours
- 48 hours

For an observation at t:

  R[h] = (Close[t+h] - Close[t]) / Close[t]

MR-03 must use only information available at t to construct the
observation. Future prices are labels used only to measure the outcome.

* Instrument Set

The first instrument is:

- EUR/USD

The same experiment will then be run without parameter changes across
a small predeclared representative basket:

- EUR/USD
- GBP/USD
- USD/JPY
- USD/CHF
- AUD/USD
- NZD/USD
- USD/CAD
- EUR/GBP
- EUR/CHF
- AUD/NZD

The basket is fixed before results are examined.

Pairs are not removed because they perform poorly, and parameters are
not changed per pair.

CHF/JPY and AUD/CAD may be reported separately as previously observed
exploratory candidates, but they must not be treated as independent
confirmation because prior exploratory analysis already identified
them.

* Historical Periods and Prior Exploration

Exploratory work has already examined approximately 2015-2025 data.

Therefore that period must not be represented as untouched
out-of-sample evidence.

The final MR-01 run configuration must distinguish:

1. data already examined during exploratory work; and
2. data that has not previously been inspected for this hypothesis.

If sufficient pre-2015 canonical H1 history is available, an untouched
pre-2015 period should be reserved as the initial holdout set.

The exact date boundaries must be recorded in this document before
MR-04 examines the event-study results.

If a genuinely untouched period cannot be obtained, MR-04 must label
the study developmental rather than claiming out-of-sample
confirmation.

* Required Measurements

For every:

- instrument;
- Z-score bucket; and
- forward horizon;

record at minimum:

- observation count;
- mean forward return;
- median forward return;
- standard deviation or comparable dispersion measure;
- fraction of observations whose return moved toward the rolling mean.

Results must retain instrument-level detail as well as any aggregate
summary.

Failures and contradictory results remain in the report.

* Expected Direction

Before observing results, the expected signs are:

| Bucket            | Expected forward-return sign |
|-------------------+------------------------------|
| Extreme negative  | Positive                     |
| Moderate negative | Weakly positive              |
| Neutral           | No directional expectation   |
| Moderate positive | Weakly negative              |
| Extreme positive  | Negative                     |

Evidence is stronger if the effect increases with |Z|.

For example:

  mean return(Z < -2) > mean return(-2 <= Z < -1)

and:

  mean return(Z > 2) < mean return(1 < Z <= 2)

This monotonic relationship is more meaningful than one isolated
profitable-looking bucket.

* Minimum Evidence Requirements

No conclusion is drawn from an extreme bucket with fewer than 30
observations for a given instrument and horizon.

Counts below that level are reported as insufficient evidence rather
than positive or negative evidence.

The primary conclusion should be based on the overall pattern across:

- both positive and negative extremes;
- multiple forward horizons;
- development and holdout periods; and
- multiple instruments.

A single statistically unusual pair/horizon combination is not enough
to establish a general FX mean-reversion effect.

Multiple comparisons must be acknowledged when many
instrument/bucket/horizon combinations are examined.

* Interpretation

MR-04 may classify the hypothesis as:

** Supported

A coherent tendency toward the mean appears across multiple
instruments and/or periods, has the expected sign, and generally
strengthens with deviation magnitude.

** Weak / Inconclusive

Some expected behavior is present, but it is inconsistent across
instruments, horizons, directions, or periods, or sample counts are
too small.

** Not Supported

Extreme deviations show no consistent tendency to revert, show
roughly random subsequent returns, or show continuation often enough
to contradict the stated hypothesis.

"Not Supported" is a successful research result. It does not trigger
parameter optimization.

* Explicit Non-Goals

MR-01 through MR-04 do not include:

- Bollinger-band strategy rules;
- RSI;
- ADX;
- ATR filters;
- trend/regime filters;
- entry or exit rules;
- stop-loss rules;
- trailing stops;
- position sizing;
- starting capital;
- transaction-cost modeling;
- broker simulation;
- parameter sweeps;
- optimization;
- machine learning;
- pair-specific parameter tuning;
- selection of only the best-performing instruments.

Those belong to later hypotheses or MR-05 and beyond if the raw
phenomenon provides sufficient evidence.

* Relationship to Later Issues

MR-02 implements only the analytical primitives required to construct
the observations defined here.

MR-03 implements the deterministic forward-return event study.

MR-04 executes this specification and records the evidence.

MR-05 is conditional on MR-04 finding enough evidence to justify
turning the statistical phenomenon into a trading strategy.

* Completion Criteria for Issue #278

Issue #278 is complete when:

- this specification is reviewed and accepted;
- the lookback, buckets, horizons, universe, and historical periods are
  frozen;
- expected signs are declared before execution;
- minimum evidence and no-evidence criteria are declared;
- previously explored data is identified honestly;
- no trading-strategy implementation has been introduced.
