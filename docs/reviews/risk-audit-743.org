#+TITLE: Risk audit: sizing, admission and simulated fills
#+SUBTITLE: Issue #743
#+DATE: [2026-11-26 Thu]
#+STARTUP: overview indent

* Verdict
The rule audited: a new position's stop, filled at its own price, loses at most
1% of the account's equity at sizing.

*Trader does not guarantee this today.* It holds for the /plan/, computed from the
order's reference price. It does not hold for the /position/, because the entry
can fill elsewhere, and the reference price itself is not checked.

No behaviour is changed by this audit. It adds findings and tests; fixes need
the owner's decisions below and separate issues. Findings describe the live root
engine at =2175c45=, not =_archive/=.

Planned risk is never a maximum realized loss: gaps, spread, slippage and closed
markets can make any exit worse.

* How an open is sized and admitted
1. The strategy proposes side, reference price and stop. It cannot name units.
   =order.validate= requires a stop on the losing side of the reference.
2. =account.sizeOpen= takes the smallest of: risk (=equity x 1% / |reference -
   stop|=), margin (equity less used margin and the margin reserved by pending
   opens), order notional (USD 50,000,000) and the instrument maximum; it rounds
   down to the unit step and rechecks with =overBudget=.
3. The broker fills on a later bar. =account.applyFill= rechecks margin and order
   notional at the fill price. *It does not recheck risk, and never resizes.*
4. A filled open places its stop. Closes need no margin.
5. After each frame the margin policy values positions at the bar's close and
   liquidates below a margin level of 50%.

Precedence: the strategy proposes; Trader sizes, admits and executes (ADR-093).
A research consumer such as Investor reads results and submits no order.

* Required checks
** Side, spread, fees, rounding
- The engine trades one price series, the mid for FX; bid and ask are dropped as
  bars enter it (=RunSeriesWith=). A long's stop triggers and fills on the mid,
  a real one on the bid; a short's on the ask. The spread at admission is not
  used, and no commission, financing or slippage exists.
- Illustration, not a measurement: a 1.3 pip round-trip spread adds about 2% to
  the planned loss of a 0.5% stop at 1.10000, and about 12% for a 0.1% stop.
- Units round down. Money is exact to 1e-8 with a pinned tolerance of 0.5e-8
  (=TestAccount_Size_Open_RiskToleranceIsPinned=). Off-tick order prices are
  refused. Bar prices are rounded to the tick. Trader never moves a stop when
  sizing.
** Account-wide margin
Used margin is at the latest mark; pending opens reserve it at their reference
price; each fill rechecks it. A later fill is refused if capacity is gone
(=TestAccount_ApplyFill_OpenChecksMarginAtTheFillPrice=). An instrument with no
bar in a frame keeps its old mark, so equity at sizing can be stale. Correlated
and shared-currency exposure is not limited. Stops only tighten
(=TestBroker_Amend_NeverLoosensAStop=). A close is admitted with no margin
available (=..._ACloseNeedsNoMargin=).
** Instruments and conversion
Backtests price EURUSD and GBPUSD only. Another symbol, or a quote currency other
than USD, stops the run with an error; nothing converts or assumes
(=TestAccount_Size_RejectsAnInstrumentQuotedInAnotherCurrency=,
=TestAccount_Size_UnknownInstrumentIsAnErrorNotARejection=).

* Owner decisions
No policy numbers are proposed here.

- D1 (F1) :: Size a market open from the decision bar's close and treat the
  strategy's price as ignored, or reject a reference that differs from it.
  *Suggested: size from the close.* The built-in EMAX already sends the close, so
  its results should not change. Limit and stop entries keep their trigger.
- D2 (F2) :: (a) accept and report; (b) size with an owner-set buffer, which
  lowers but cannot guarantee the loss; (c) reduce units at the fill to the
  budget, never increase; (d) reject at the fill when risk exceeds the budget.
  *Suggested: (c) for simulation only if the owner accepts that a live broker
  cannot know its fill in advance; otherwise (b).* (c) changes the rule that an
  order is never resized, and changes existing results.
- D3 (F3, F4) :: Add a cap on position or gross notional as a multiple of equity,
  independent of stop distance and of broker margin. *Suggested: yes*, per order
  and account-wide, with no default until the owner sets one; per ADR-093 an
  unset required control must block, not mean unlimited.
- D4 (F5) :: Add an aggregate planned-loss limit across positions and pending
  opens. Needed before trading several instruments or paper trading.

Related: a proposal to allow opens without a stop (discussion #742) would remove
the risk limit for those opens; D3 should be settled first.
